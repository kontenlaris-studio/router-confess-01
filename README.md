One line, 132 characters: a home Wi-Fi router excuses itself for dropping the connection at the exact moment a movie reaches its climax.

> The movie froze at the worst line because I did — 9 seconds of red light, then darkness, then your scream found me through the wall.

## Why this line

- One filmable scene: the movie freezes, the router dies, the human screams through the wall.
- Hyper-specific number: 9 seconds of red light — the router counts its own death.
- Real mechanism → absurd: the router's death *is* the movie freezing, and the dead router still hears the scream.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
